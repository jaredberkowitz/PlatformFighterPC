# Art review 7: what still reads flat (2026-10-18)

Owner's direction: make the stages feel less flat and less like a Flash game, more refined and polished, by refining what is there
(depth, form, light), not by adding detail. Match-camera shots of every stage: `docs/screenshots/review7_all_stages.jpg`
(meadow, canyon, beach, grove, city, top to bottom).

The lesson from the city windows (art review 6 → 7): painted depth never convinces; what convinced was real geometry standing out
from the surface, lit on its top edges and casting a shadow. The biggest flat areas left are the same kind of thing.

## Ranked by screen area × flatness

1. **The stage fronts (every stage, 30–40% of the screen).** Each is one box with a painted tile: the meadow's red earth and the
   grove's orange earth with drawn roots, the canyon's wavy strata, the beach's sea-wall bricks. They are the largest thing on screen
   and the flattest. Fix: build their relief as geometry, as the city windows were.
   - Beach: the bricks as bevelled blocks standing slightly proud of dark mortar (MultiMesh), lit on top, each a little off true.
   - Canyon: each stratum bed a shallow ledge stepping out over the one below, lit on its top lip, shaded under it.
   - Meadow and grove: the earth as a few large rounded bulges and the stones/roots as real shapes bedded in it, so the face has a
     silhouette and turns into shade at the bottom; the drawn roots become a few modelled roots.
2. **The stage's top edge (every stage).** A straight box edge with a flat lip (the meadow's drip moss, the grove's lime strip, the sand
   caps). Fix: a rounded, slightly overhanging lip with a lit top and a shadow line under it, its outline a little uneven.
3. **The floating platforms (every stage, centre of the view).** Thin flat slabs: the meadow's yellow top with flat red sides, the
   canyon's and grove's plain boards. Fix: built planks with gaps and bevelled ends, a beam under them, the side faces showing grain and
   falling into shade, the grove's vines as modelled clumps.
4. **Meadow background.** The mountain range is one teal mass with flat bands; the sky islands are pale flat slabs; the lawn top is a
   saturated flat lime. Fix: lit ridges and crevasses on the mountains, the islands' undersides in real shade, the lawn top a little
   less saturated with its patches stronger.
5. **Beach.** The sea is flat bands of blue; palm leaves are flat green cards; the umbrella is a flat disc. Fix: the sea's swell lit and
   shaded toward the horizon, leaves folded along a midrib (lit half and shaded half), the umbrella's panels ribbed.
6. **Grove.** The great trunk's bark is drawn stripes on a smooth cylinder. Fix: bark plates with real relief on the trunk's silhouette
   and lit ridges.
7. **Canyon.** The sand top and the distant hills are flat fills; the mesas read well. Fix: wind ripples in the sand catching the low sun,
   the far hills lit along their crests.
8. **City.** Better after this round; left: the neon trim strip under the roof edge is a flat yellow band.

## Suggested order

1 (fronts) and 3 (platforms) first, one stage at a time starting with the beach (its bricks map most directly to the window method),
then 2, then the per-stage backgrounds (4–8). Each step: screenshot before and after at the match camera, owner review.
