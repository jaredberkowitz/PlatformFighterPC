# Art review 10: the whole game against the genre's leaders (2026-10-18)

The owner's question: we keep making small stage gains that are hard to notice. Where are the big gaps? Compared against the big
crossover fighter and the newest indie platform fighter (the camera reference and the competitor of docs/COMPETITOR_GAP.md), scored 1 to 5
(5 = on par). Captures: menu, fighter select, HUD, the fighter lineup, a CPU fight on the meadow, the four stages.

| Category | Score | What the leaders do that we don't |
|---|---|---|
| Animation and posing | 2 | Strong key poses, anticipation, follow-through, squash and stretch, smears; every move reads as a shape. Ours are stiff and mid-pose most frames, which is a big part of the "Flash game" feel. |
| Fighter models and materials | 2 | Distinct silhouettes, layered materials (cloth, metal, skin, hair), crisp rim light. Ours are one smooth body in one flat colour, so at play distance they read as coloured jellybeans with small hats. |
| Hit effects and game feel | 3 | Big, bold, coloured hit sparks sized to the hit, launch trails, KO blasts, impact frames. Ours are thin white slashes and small sparks that get lost on bright stages. |
| Cohesion of style | 2.5 | One visual language everywhere. Ours mixes three: glossy vinyl fighters, painted and faceted stages, brush-slash UI. |
| Stage play area (the platforms, walls and tops) | 3.5 | Built, readable, lit. Ours are close after reviews 7 to 9. |
| Stage backgrounds | 3 | Few, large, calm forms that frame the fight. Ours are busy: many props and colours at the same strength. |
| Lighting and rendering | 3.5 | Clean and bright. Ours are fine technically; the paper trial (F10) is pending feedback. |
| UI and HUD | 3.5 | Polished and animated. Ours are clear and in style; select-screen cards are small and plain. |

## Where the biggest wins are

The stages have had nine review rounds; the fighters, which are on screen and in focus 100% of the time, have had almost none. The three
biggest noticeable wins are all about the fighters:

1. **Animation and posing** (the largest gap). It is on hold by the owner's choice; it is the next big project when animation reopens
   (docs/SATURDAY_BACKLOG.md phase 2).
2. **Fighter look** within the one-shared-body rule: a material and shading pass (a toy-vinyl finish with a crisp rim and a soft
   specular, a darker separated shade on the limbs, cloth and gear in their own materials), and bolder silhouette pieces (bigger hats,
   hair, capes and weapons that change the outline), so each fighter is recognisable as a shape at play distance.
3. **Hit effects**: bigger, coloured, layered hit sparks scaled by hit strength, a short white impact frame on strong hits, launch
   trails and a KO burst.

Then: **one style**, decided by the F10 trial, and **calmer backgrounds** (fewer, larger forms, quieter colour behind the fight).
