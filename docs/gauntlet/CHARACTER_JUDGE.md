# Character judge's brief (2026-10-19)

The fighter models only, not the animations. Score against the owner's character reference, `docs/art/reference_character_style.webp`
(four round-headed scouts in a vehicle: the yellow sailor, the orange aviator scout, the blue holiday shirt, the pink ranger), and the
notes in `docs/art/characters/CHARACTER_ANALYSIS.md`.

## Each round gets
- A turnaround of the fighter being worked on (front, three-quarter, side), the reference crop of its counterpart beside it, the default
  lineup, and the fighter in a match at a close camera on two stages (one bright, one dark), with greyscale and a before/after.

## What to judge, in order
1. **Silhouette and proportion**: head size and shape (a broad squat oval sitting on the shoulders, no neck), body block, limb weight,
   hands and feet; read at match size first, close up second.
2. **Shapes on the body**: hats, hair, collars, sleeves, cuffs, socks, sashes, badges, glasses; their volume and how far they stand off
   the body. This is where the reference's detail lives.
3. **Material**: matte cel shading with a definite shadow side, the shade a deeper version of each colour; soft painted texture where
   the reference has it (fleece, cotton, knit, enamel); no gloss.
4. **Face**: thin confident ink lines, small plain pupils, an attitude of its own per fighter.
5. **Readability**: each fighter at least 0.05 from the stage ground in greyscale, distinct from the others in colour and outline.
6. **Polish tells**: seams, clipping, floating pieces, gaps at the wrists or neck, outline artefacts.

## Report
- A score out of 5 against the reference and a ship-ready score out of 5 (would it pass in a released indie platform fighter).
- The five biggest gaps, ranked, each naming the exact part and a concrete fix, tagged with effort (S/M/L) and whether it is
  geometry, material, face or proportion.
- Closer or further than the last round, and why in one line.
- What already matches the reference (so it is kept).
Owner rulings: animation is out of scope; the base body is shared by all fighters, so a body change must suit every outfit.
