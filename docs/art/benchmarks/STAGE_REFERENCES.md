# Per-stage references (the owner, 2026-10-18)

Image files: `harbour_reference.*` and `city_reference.*` in this folder once added (the owner's screenshots). Described here so the
judge can use them either way.

## Harbour (our beach / dock stage)

A stylised harbour seen from a low camera over a wooden pier (a painterly 3D game):
- **Wood:** wide, chunky planks in warm honey tones, each one visibly its own board, with painted grain, chipped and rounded ends and
  edges, a soft lit top edge and darker gaps. The planks fan in a cross shape and are not a uniform grid. The supports are short, thick
  posts with a pale waterline band and soft ripple rings in the water round them.
- **Props:** a red-brown wooden crane with iron bolts and a thick rope with real twist, and slatted crates with cross-braced sides
  piled with fruit. Barrels are banded in metal. Everything is chunky, bevelled, hand-made in feel, and nothing is a primitive.
- **Water:** clear turquoise, lighter near the pier with visible depth, and soft painted ripple highlights in place of hard foam lines.
- **Distance:** the town across the water is small and low, a dense band of pastel houses at the waterline. The hills behind are soft,
  hazy, blue-green and layered, with a distant statue on a peak. The town reads as far away and calm.
- **Light:** soft, warm daylight with gentle shadows. The value range is moderate, nothing harsh, and there are no ink outlines.

## City (our city rooftop stage)

The crossover fighter's skyscraper-rooftop stage:
- **The play building:** a pale stone art-deco tower in stepped tiers with gold-framed windows, crisp lit top edges on every ledge, and
  red beacon lights at the corners. The fighting surface is a warm terracotta roof. Two small red-railed platforms stick out from the
  sides.
- **Background:** dozens of skyscrapers in pale greys, lilacs and blue-whites, crisp but receding into a bright blue haze. They are
  daytime and high-key: the background is lighter than the play building, which stands out by being warmer and more detailed. The
  windows are fine regular grids that read as texture, not as busy shapes.
- **Light:** bright daylight, cool sky ambient, clean shading, soft atmospheric perspective.
- **Lesson for ours:** our night city keeps everything in one dark purple band. The reference separates its layers by value and
  temperature (warm, detailed foreground; pale, hazy, cool distance) and gives the play building crisp lit edges.

## Meadow (the owner, 2026-10-19): `papercraft_mountains_reference.png`

The papercraft adventure's blue-mountain camp, the meadow's target for colour, light, depth and cohesion (not its paper look):
- **Where the quality comes from:** forms and light, almost no texture. Mountains are big faceted planes, each one clean tone, lit
  planes pale and warm, shaded planes teal, layered lighter toward the peaks. Pines are stacked faceted cones in deep green.
- **Ground:** nearly flat bright lime, shaded softly by the shape of the land; paths one warm sand tone; crisp lit edges on every ledge.
- **Light:** high-key, one clear sun, soft cool shade; contact shadows under everything.
- **Depth:** foreground greens and warm sand, mid-ground deep pines, distance cool teal and pale; each layer simpler than the one in front.
