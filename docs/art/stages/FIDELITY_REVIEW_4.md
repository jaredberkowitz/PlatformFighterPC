# Stage fidelity review 4 (2026-10-21, after the world-building, the paper houses and the lit-bevel scenery)

The owner, stepping back from the details: a full analysis of every stage, everything scored 1 to 5, the pain points ranked. The scale is
review 3's (5 = hard to tell apart in quality from the references, the owner's bar), the categories finer, so the backgrounds and the world
can be scored apart from the play area. The references are the same (docs/art/benchmarks/, and the papercraft screenshots in
docs/art/stages/LOOK.md).

**Evidence:** every stage as it ships (build playtest-2026-10-21-sim146-background) at the match camera and wide, measured in review 3's
regions (value / contrast / saturation), and compared with review 3.

**Since review 3:** the paper prop kit and printed surfaces on every stage; the full paper finish on the fronts; world-building on every
stage, its life and motion, then the owner's notes (no animals, the far tree village); the paper houses at the characters' proportions;
the scenery without ink (lit bevels); the world pieces rebuilt in the props' construction.

## What the numbers say (review 3 → now, value / contrast at the match camera)

- **Meadow:** the lawn lost form, contrast 0.122 → 0.076 at value 0.72 (the scenery's ink drew its creases); the distance is softer
  (0.060 → 0.042); the striped hills behind are the busiest region (contrast 0.130).
- **Grove:** the lawn is pale and washed (0.79, saturation 0.28); the abyss under the island one dark flat band (0.33).
- **Canyon:** the front is very dark and saturated (0.30, saturation 0.60) and fills the lower half; the butte behind the fight still
  sits at the fighters' value (0.52).
- **City:** the towers behind the fight are the most contrasty background on any stage (0.155), the sky dark and saturated (0.38, 0.57).
- **Beach:** within 0.02 of review 3 everywhere.

## The order (gain against effort)

1. **The background land** (1): the meadow's patchwork first (mocked), then the grove's terraces and abyss, then the canyon's floor.
   Highest gain left; one method across the three.
2. **Card clouds** (3): mocked, cheap, every stage.
3. **Cardboard buttes** (2): the canyon's biggest gain; with item 1's canyon floor it rebuilds the canyon's middle distance.
4. **A light pass** (4, 5, 8): form on the lawns, a mood for the beach (late afternoon), coloured rather than grey distance. Settings
   and shader work, every stage.
5. **The city's backdrop** (6): quieter towers behind the play area, the scaffolds remodelled.
6. Smaller: the canyon's front a step lighter and calmer (7); the trestle lower or broken by the buttes (9).
