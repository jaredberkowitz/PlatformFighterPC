Stage by stage

Meadow mean 3.7 to 3.8

Lighting moved: the distance lighter and softer (0.59 to 0.67, contrast 0.091 to 0.060), shade under the hay bales and the lawn's lip, cut edges on the island and platforms. What remains: flat colour up close, and the hay bales, fence, barrel and sign are still code-built.

Grove mean 3.5 to 3.8

The lanterns and their pools, the lawn lighter and calmer (0.67 to 0.76) and the mid-ground now dark terraces (0.50 to 0.33): the fight reads first. What remains: plain surfaces, and the mushrooms, bushes and stump are code-built.

Canyon mean 3.3 to 3.7

The sand paler and cooler (0.65 to 0.74, saturation 0.53 to 0.38), so the orange fighter no longer stands on its own hue; sheet shadows on the mesas. What remains: the butte behind the fight near the fighters (0.52), the flat floor, code-built rocks and cacti.

City mean 3.2 to 3.5

The searchlights, and the roof now reads first (0.40 to 0.63) over a front at 0.30. What remains: the solid brick front under the paper skyline, the darkest fighter on the dark backdrop (a fighter-side fix), and the water tower, scaffolds and pipes are code-built.

Beach mean 3.5, unchanged

The one stage that did not move: the distance a little softer and the deck lighter, but its light is still flat midday, and the ripple rings show mostly in the wide view. The deck and timber are still plain.
