Built · the paper house kit (your pick: B, layered card)

Paper houses for the far towns, at true size

The meadow's village and farm and the beach's harbour town repeat one cottage model from before the paper look (fifteen times over in the town). The kit replaces it with four paper houses (a cottage, a two-storey townhouse, a longhouse and a barn) built at true size: a door is about a fighter's height. Three styles: A scored card (crisp folds, scored roof rows, card frames), B layered card (applied timber strips, shutters, flower boxes, overlapping roof rows), C rolled (corners, frames, eaves and ridges as rolled tubes, arched doors, as your props).

The scale and footing pass: the houses, the town's warehouse and bell tower and the haystacks are life size. The meadow's farm stays at 0.6: it stands on the valley's painted face, a forced-perspective layer whose fields and hedges shrink down it, and at life size it filled the frame's corner. The rule for that face: whatever stands on it takes its scale. Every house sits on a footing sunk into its ground; none floats. Details in docs/art/stages/HOUSES.md.

My pick: B. At play distance its shutters and flower boxes read as lived-in; A is crisp but plain, and C's rolled edges are lost (its arched doors remain).
