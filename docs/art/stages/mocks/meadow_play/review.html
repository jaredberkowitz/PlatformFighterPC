Meadow pilot: the play area in paper, and the paper finish

Picked: B (layered sheets with the finish), now in the game. Before and after: docs/art/stages/meadow_play_built.png.

The lawn you fight on and the two platforms as paper card, modelled in Blender on the collision (every walking surface stays exactly where it was), each with a level of the new paper finish on all the scenery: a faint fibre grain, a slight colour wobble from sheet to sheet and, on B and C, a pale cut edge. Fighters are never touched. The front and the paper background are unchanged. Pick a view, then click an option (or press 1 to 4). All four shows them side by side.

A is the calmest; C's ground row hides the lawn's edge and its pale platforms read a little blank. The fighters' contact shadows and the lawn's colour are kept in all three.
