Mood light in paper

Light as cut paper, for the three stages where it suits the setting: searchlight beams over the city, paper lanterns in the grove, ripple rings round the beach's posts. Each piece carries its own glow and fade, sits behind or beside the play area and never covers a fighter. Choose a stage, a view and an option; press 1 to 4 to switch options in place. The wide view is the camera pulled back, as when the fighters spread out; on the beach the match camera crops the dock's own waterline, so its rings show round the boardwalk behind it.
