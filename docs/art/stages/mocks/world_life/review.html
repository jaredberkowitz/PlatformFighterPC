Life: the world-building moves

The places have their reasons and their signs of life; now they move, gently and on their own clock, the way the windmill's sails, the clouds and the city's cars already do. Nothing moves near the fight. Two new things join: hawks over the canyon and fireflies in the grove. Each clip is 8 seconds from the match camera, looping. A keeps everything in its place (bobbing, wheeling, grazing, fluttering, curling smoke, twinkling); B adds the travellers (the canyon's train, the city's bus and people). My pick: B, because the train crossing is the canyon's biggest gain and the bus joins the city's own car traffic.
