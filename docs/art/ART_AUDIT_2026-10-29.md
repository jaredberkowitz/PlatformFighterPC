## Weakest spots now, ranked
1. **Fighters are small at the match camera (presentation, 3).**
   - The fighters fill about 16% of the frame's height in a 1v1, close or apart, and the camera holds the whole stage.
   - Most of this month's character and animation work shows only at select-screen size. In a match, what reads is silhouette, colour and the swing arcs.
   - **Correction (same day):** today's framing was set on purpose to the reference game's close exchange, a fighter about a sixth of
     the screen (main.gd `CAMERA_NEAREST`, 26). A closer camera (19) was tried before and dropped, because the main stage no longer
     fitted and every speed read faster than the reference's. So this is a trade-off between detail and the stage fitting and the
     speed reading right, not a defect.
   - **Suggested:** mock two or three tighter 1v1 framings for the owner to compare with today's, e.g. a closer zoom when the fighters
     are near, the same as today when they spread, and blast zones still shown before a KO. This is a feel call, so mock it and leave
     the camera as it is until the owner picks.
2. **Attack animation: limb bends, hidden arms, weight (3.5).** The owner's notes of 10-29 cover it, and the animator has them as its next groups:
   - no arms behind or into the body, and no wrong-way bends;
   - no weightlessness;
   - the Longsword's hidden arm, neutral air and sword length.

   The snap/sweep call (F10) also gates part of the fluidity.
3. **Roster identity (3.5).**
   - The same maroon sash on every default fighter.
   - Three aviator caps among 14 fighters.
   - The default sailor and aviator pair in most matches.

   The mock is built (art/identity: five signature hats and a re-dressed roster) and needs only the owner's review. It is the cheapest big gain on this list.
4. **The canyon stage (4).** Close to one hue, with big plain blocks in the play area front. The stage session has it in review 15, which also covers the grove's busy foreground and plain lawn.
5. **Controls, Online and Replays screens (3.5).** Long uniform bar lists. Group them under clear headings, give each list a selected-item preview or detail panel, and mute the unselected rows.
6. **The creator's eye choices (4).** The eight tiles look alike at tile size. Draw each tile larger, cropped to the eyes.
7. **The shield bubble.** A glassy sphere in an otherwise paper game. Give it the paper treatment: a cut-paper disc with the player's colour and a lit edge.
