(4 to 4.5) is still ahead of the motion, but the gap has halved.

## Weakest visuals now, ranked
1. **The punch smear reads as a glass tube** (effects, `godot/scripts/smear.gd` with the paper look). It is new since the paper effects
   pass and visible on every punch-type strike. Fix: draw the ribbon facing the camera (it is built in the swing plane), thinner, in
   the class colour without the cream core.
2. **The idle still reads upright at match distance** for the sword family. The knees bend, but the head and chest stay high. Push the
   chest down and forward a further step, measured at the match camera, not in the contact sheet.
3. **The canyon's buttes and its floating top platform** (stage session's area).
4. **The walk** is one shared cycle that slides about 0.45 of its speed (`ANIMATION.md` section 6). It is the last unreworked
   locomotion clip.
5. **Stand-in poses** on the Bracers and Popgun specials. The Dagger's cocked knife pokes above the hat, and the Needles' up tilt leg
   points at the camera (a body-proportion limit).
6. **Springs on real time:** hitlag doesn't freeze the squash, lean and hat springs, so a strong hit's freeze still wobbles.
7. **Roster identity:** a signature silhouette piece per built-in fighter (bodies and sashes still match).

## Suggested next
1. Fix the smear tube (effects; small).
2. An animation round 2 from the list above: the idle at the match camera, the walk per family, the stand-in poses and hitlag-frozen
   springs.
3. The canyon's buttes and platform.
4. Roster identity.

The run mode stays deferred.
