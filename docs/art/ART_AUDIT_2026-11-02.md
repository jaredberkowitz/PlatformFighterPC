# Game-wide art audit (2026-11-02)

Everything players see except the run mode, rated 1 to 5 (5: would pass in a released indie platform fighter). Same scale as the audits of
10-21, 10-24, 10-25, 10-26 and 10-29. The run mode is still left out by the owner's choice.

This audit has one centre: **how the fighters read at the match camera**. The owner's ranking of 11-02 (relayed by the stage session) named
the three biggest gains left, all in the fighters: reading at match size, attack weight and limb bends, and roster identity.

**Build captured:** the installed build, animation core4 (889008d9, simulation version 147). It holds:
- the max-effort animation pass through core4: idles, runs, jumps, dodges and the forward roll's turn;
- the character merge f51e7418: hair, capes and hoods, the cloth fixes, bare legs and no belts;
- stage pass 17 and the grove's distance (stage-refine de358471);
- the paper menus.

**Captures** (all on F:\art_review):
- seven CPU matches at 1920x1080 with the match HUD only, covering all 14 classes on all five stages;
- the 14 built-in fighters lined up four at a time at the match camera's nearest distance;
- greyscale and half-size versions of both.

Summary sheets are in `docs/art/audit6/`. The reference game's match footage was compared the same way; those frames stay on F: and are
not in the repo.

**The other scales running alongside this one:**
- The stage session's fidelity review 17: 4.98 on its own scale.
- The animator's Animation Review 2: 34 shared clips, mean 3.40, scored against the reference fighters.

## Weakest spots now, ranked

### 1. The fighters' values at match size (3)
- **What the captures show:** in greyscale, our fighters are a flat mid grey on mid-grey stages. They separate from the stage mostly by hue,
  outline and contact shadow (`match_value.jpg`: each stage in colour, then in greyscale).
- **The roster in greyscale** (`roster_match_size.jpg`, in roster order Duelist, Brawler, Rogue, Tumbler, Sprinter, Seer, Champion,
  Hauler, Bounder, Wanderer, Settler, Scamp, Phantom, Exile; rows: colour, greyscale, half size, half size in greyscale):
  - only three carry a large dark area: the Champion (a dark body), the Phantom (a black body and top hat) and the Rogue (a dark hood);
  - the other eleven are light or mid grey from head to foot;
  - at half size (the pulled-back camera), those eleven dissolve into the floor, while the three dark ones still stand out.
- **The reference does the opposite:** high-key, hazy backgrounds behind fighters who are dark masses, with a light accent or two. In
  greyscale they read instantly even when small.
- **The stage session's measure agrees** (review 17, player 1's default fighter): since the bright floors, the value gap to the floor is
  0.10 to 0.17. On the meadow (0.107) and the grove (0.099) it is under the 0.12 that reads by value alone, so there the fighter reads by
  colour.
- **Correction to a note I gave the owner during this audit:** the ink outline does not thin when the camera pulls back. Its width is held
  at about 4 pixels on screen at every distance by design (`outline.gdshader`), so it isn't part of this problem.

### 2. Match camera framing (3)
Unchanged, by the owner's choice. It is why point 1 matters so much: the fighters are small, so their values have to do the work.

### 3. Attacks (3.5)
Weight in the wind-up, impact and settle, the remaining elbows, and the timing refit after the rig rebuild. This is the animator's main job
after the hit reactions. At match size the strikes read through the effects more than through the bodies, so the lines of action must show
in silhouette: limbs held clear of the body, and the striking hand separating from the torso at the hit.

### 4. Roster identity (3.5)
- **The shared base:** fourteen fighters share one body, one stance and, mostly, one pair of olive shorts. At a glance they are told apart
  by body colour and headgear.
  - Strong: the Duelist (helm and cape), the Seer (crown and staff), the Champion (spiky hair and greatsword), the Phantom (top hat and cape)
    and the Exile (conical hat).
  - Weakest: the Hauler (tan with brown hair and the olive shorts), the Tumbler and the Bounder.
- **Hue against the stage:** the orange-skinned fighters on the beach's orange boardwalk are close in hue as well as in value, so they
  nearly vanish (`match_value.jpg`, beach row).

### 5. Controls, Online, Replays (3.5)
Not re-checked here. Still lists with little hierarchy.

## What to do next

Items 1 and 4 are character work (the art director). Item 3 is the animator's, already in progress. Mocks for the owner to pick from,
all presentation only, with no change to the sim or content:

- **A. A dark area on every fighter.** Each built-in fighter gets one dark mass in a deep shade of its own palette: the shorts, the shirt,
  the hat or the hair.
  - The shared olive shorts become per-fighter colours, which helps identity too.
  - Each fighter keeps one light accent.
  - Target: in greyscale every fighter shows a dark, a mid and a light area, and still separates from the floor at half size.
- **B. A pale cut edge.** A cream rim along the lit side of each fighter's silhouette, the same pale cut-paper edge the stages' fronts
  carry. It separates a fighter from any background value and ties the fighters into the paper look.
- **C. Both.**

Each mock to be captured on the roster lineup and on three stages (meadow, beach, grove), in colour and in greyscale, at full and half
size.

**For the stage session (its call):**
- the beach boardwalk's orange against the orange-skinned fighters;
- the grove's forest behind the play area, the busiest background behind fighters.

The stage side could help item 1 too, with a little more haze or less value contrast in the mid-ground behind the play area, as the
reference's backgrounds have.
