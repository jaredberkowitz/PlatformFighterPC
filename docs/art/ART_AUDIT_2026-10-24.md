# Game-wide art audit (2026-10-24)

Everything you can see in the game, rated 1 to 5 (5: would pass in a released indie platform fighter). This round includes animation,
which the 2026-10-21 audit (`ART_AUDIT_2026-10-21.md`) left out.

**What was captured:** the build installed in the owner's game folder today, branch `build/art-2026-10-21b` (2e6a812). It holds main
(sim 146), the stage refinement, the character pass and the paper effects. Two summary sheets are committed in `docs/art/audit2/`:
`stages_and_select.png` (the five stages and fighter select) and `animation_sheets.png` (the sword fighter's idle, run, forward smash,
neutral air and hurt, and the claws fighter's forward air). The full set of 154 MB stayed local. They include:
- every menu, the five stages, the HUD, the pause screen and the results;
- the cast in a beauty shot, turnarounds and the roster;
- hits, KOs and the small effects;
- the run mode;
- contact sheets of the sword (class 0) and claws (class 1) fighters' movement, reactions and attacks (`tests/anim_sheet.gd`).

**Overall: 3.7** (3.5 on 10-21). The still image is now well ahead of the motion. Stages, UI and the model are 4 to 4.5 while animation
sits at 2.5. Playtesters see fighters in motion, so **animation is the biggest gap in the game**, followed by the run mode (deferred until the rest of the game is closer to final).

## What to work on next
1. **Animation overhaul.** It is the lowest-scoring area that is on screen in every match. The brief for its own agent is
   `docs/ANIMATION_OVERHAUL_AGENT.md`.
2. **Run mode art pass (2.5): deferred.** The owner (2026-10-25) holds it until the rest of the game is closer to final.
3. **Small items between rounds:**
   - the canyon's buttes and its floating top platform;
   - bare platform slabs on the city and meadow;
   - the creator's eye choices;
   - the Controls, Online and Replays screens.
4. **Roster identity:** a signature silhouette piece per built-in fighter. Do it after the animation per-family sets, since those also
   separate the classes.
