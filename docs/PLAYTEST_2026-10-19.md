## Dodges (the owner's follow-up: do rolls have intangibility, and do the numbers match?)

They do, but every dodge's intangibility came **one frame late** (a roll's 4-15 was 5-16, the spot dodge's 3-17 was 4-18, the air dodge's
3-29 was 4-30): the windows were read against a frame count that starts one later than the reference's. Fixed for all three. The
backward roll now has its own window (most bodies 5-16 against 4-15 forward; the rogue 4-14, the scamp 4-15). The totals matched for
ten bodies; the four later ports (settler, scamp, phantom, exile) had every dodge total one frame long (rolls, two spot dodges, all four
air dodge lengths), from reading the reference's totals as first actionable frames. All 14 now match (`playtest_fixes.rs` holds the
table). **Done, sim 144.**

## The floaty feel (the owner, after the footage pass)

The measured jumps, drift, launches and the full hop's opening all match the reference (`docs/FOOTAGE_COMPARISON.md`), and the
group plays on controller, so the leading suspect is the camera: it rose with every jump (about 44% of a jump's height, lagging
behind), flattening how high and fast jumps look. Now a Display setting, **Camera: Steady** (the default) or **Follow**, also switchable
from the pause screen ("View") to compare in play: steady counts a fighter in the air at the height it stood on until it is 5.5
units above it (over any full hop), so ordinary jumps leave the view still while launches and recoveries still move it
(`godot/tests/camera_test.gd`). Next suspects if this is not it: the animations (no crouch before a jump, no landing squash) and the
display delay.
